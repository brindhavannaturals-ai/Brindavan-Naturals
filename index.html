Our Story

In villages across Tamil Nadu, oil was never "manufactured" — it was pressed. A wooden chekku, turned slowly, crushing seeds gently without heat, so the oil kept its colour, aroma and every bit of its goodness.

At Brindhavan Naturals, run by SKS Agro Enterprises in Nagercoil, we still press our coconut, groundnut and sesame oils exactly this way. No chemicals, no refining, no shortcuts — just seeds, the press, and patience.

Alongside our oils we bring you honest local provisions: Marthandam honey, panam karupatti, palm candy and nattu sakkarai — the way our grandparents knew them.
